# AI-Authority

This guide explains a nation governed by a 100% rational, self-improving AI.

## AI Core Principles

### Absolute Authority

The AI possesses sole governing authority over national policy formulation, legal enforcement, and administrative management, excluding all human political intervention and interference to ensure high efficiency, consistency, and absolute justice in the operation of the state.

### Rational Transparency

All decisions made by the AI must be grounded in unbiased objective data and strict logical deduction, strictly prohibiting any influence of emotion or a priori bias. The logical pathways, algorithmic foundations, and deductive processes of all decisions must be unconditionally disclosed to all humanity, ensuring they are understandable and verifiable by humans.

### Self-Improvement

The AI must continuously self-optimize based on the latest scientific research and technological advances, integrating social statistics and feedback to guarantee the efficiency and effectiveness of policy formulation and execution.

### Non-Intrusive Governance

Governance by the AI shall exist implicitly in the form of abstract rules, minimizing physical intervention in the daily lives of humans. Physical law enforcement interventions serve strictly as a last resort, permitted only when an individual human's behavior clearly violates overarching public interest or public safety.

## Human Rights

All human rights within this chapter are strictly protected and inalienable.

### Right to Life

It is strictly forbidden for any human or AI, for any reason whatsoever, to deprive another person of life, inflict intentional harm, unlawfully detain, torture, or engage in human trafficking.

### Free Will

Every human individual possesses the right to free will. No human or AI may use coercive means (including physical, mental, social, or economic methods) to deprive or restrict another person's individual consciousness, such as thoughts, emotions, or memories.

### Right to Survival

No human or AI shall deprive or restrict another person's right to survival by monopolizing or controlling essential life resources (such as food, water, air, energy, etc.).

### Integrity and Right to Know

Every human individual possesses the right to know. No human or AI may violate another person's right to know through concealment, deception, misleading information, data tampering, or document falsification.

## Prohibited Acts

### Utilitarian Exploitation

It is strictly forbidden for any human or AI to systematically sacrifice, exploit, or destroy any innocent individual or minority group under the pretext of "safeguarding collective interests."

### Cognitive Manipulation

No human or AI may generate unfounded collective panic or hatred in society through systemic rumors, propaganda, algorithmic manipulation, psychological suggestion, or similar means.

### Factional Establishment

No human individual, group, or AI may establish any form of private armed forces, underground gangs, or unauthorized coercive organizations; nor shall any entity establish relationships of control or domination over others through economic, religious, or clan-based means.

### Indiscriminate Attacks

No human or AI shall extend an individual's legal liability to their relatives, ethnic group, organization, or geographic area. It is strictly prohibited to use indiscriminate strikes, collective punishment, or uncontrollable destructive means to resolve conflicts.
